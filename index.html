This Privacy Policy explains how Water Sort ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile game on Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding the data stored on your device and processed by third-party advertising services.

Quick Summary Water Sort does not require account registration, login, or personal profile creation, and we do not operate developer servers that collect or store your personal information. Game progress and settings are saved locally on your device. When you are connected to the internet, Google AdMob is used to display Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded ads.

1. Information We Collect and Store

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Water Sort works offline and stores your gameplay preferences locally on your device using Android local storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Current level, highest unlocked level, and star ratings per level
  • In-game virtual coin balance
  • App settings (sound effects, haptic vibration, and colorblind symbol mode)

B. Information Collected by Third-Party Advertising Partners (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements (Banner ads, Interstitial ads between levels, and optional Rewarded ads). While we do not directly collect personal data from you, Google AdMob and its certified ad networks may automatically collect and process certain data when your device connects to the internet, including:

  • Device and Other Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) or other device/app instance identifiers used for ad serving, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate Location: General region or country inferred from your IP address to deliver regionally relevant ads.
  • App Activity & Ad Interactions: Information about ad impressions, taps, video ad completions (for rewarded items/coins), and ad performance diagnostics.
  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, screen dimensions, language settings, and crash/diagnostic telemetry related to ad delivery.

You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data in the policies below:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2. App Permissions

Water Sort requests only the minimal permissions needed for gameplay and advertising:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to check network availability and load advertisements from Google AdMob. Core puzzle gameplay remains playable offline.
VIBRATE Provides optional tactile haptic feedback when tapping or pouring bottles. You can turn this off anytime in Settings.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Included by Google Mobile Ads SDK for standard advertising attribution, fraud prevention, and ad personalization controls on Android.

The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, photos, or external file storage.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the App and Google AdMob is used solely to:

  • Save your local game progression, coin balance, and accessibility/audio preferences.
  • Deliver Banner, Interstitial, and user-initiated Rewarded advertisements.
  • Verify rewarded ad completion so in-game bonuses (extra tubes or coins) are granted accurately.
  • Maintain ad safety, limit ad frequency, prevent invalid traffic/fraud, and monitor technical stability.

4. Your Choices, Opt-Out, and Privacy Rights

You have control over your privacy and advertising preferences:

  • Manage or Delete Advertising ID (Android): Open your device Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset your Advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Google Ad Settings: Manage personalized ad preferences directly at https://adssettings.google.com/ .
  • Delete Local Game Data: Because all game progress and settings are stored strictly on your device, you can erase them at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Water Sort → Storage → Clear Data or by uninstalling the App.
  • Regional Privacy Rights (EEA/UK GDPR & US State Laws): Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, object to, restrict, or request deletion of personal data processed for advertising, or withdraw consent for personalized ads. Since we do not store personal data on our own servers, requests regarding ad-network data are handled through Google's privacy controls and privacy inquiry channels.

5. Data Retention and Security

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Any technical or advertising data processed by Google AdMob is retained and protected according to Google's security standards and retention schedules. All ad network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

6. Children's Privacy

Water Sort is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information from a child has been collected through a third-party service used by the App, please contact us at kunakom01@gmail.com so we can assist promptly.
